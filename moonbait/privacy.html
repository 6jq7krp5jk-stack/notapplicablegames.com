Moonbait privacy

What the game sends off your phone, and what we do with it.

Last updated November 18, 2026

The short version

Moonbait plays offline and keeps your game on your phone. It backs up your save to our server so you don't lose it, with no sign-up. It only sends how you play if you say yes. No ads, no tracking, and we don't sell anything about you.

What leaves your phone

When you're online, the game backs up your save to our server as a guest. You don't need to sign up. To stop it, go to Settings › Account and tap Delete my data. That also deletes your backups. Your game stays on your phone, and you can turn the backup on again in the same place.

With the backup on, the game sends:

  • An account number. The game makes one for you. It isn't your name, your email or your phone's ID.
  • An install number. A random number, so we can tell your phones apart. You get a new one when you delete your data.
  • Your save. So you can get it back on a new phone. It includes anything you bought with real money.

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, Apple gives us a number for you and a key we use only to remove that link when you delete your data. We don't ask Apple for your name or email.

If you buy something, Apple or Google tells RevenueCat (see below) what you bought and when, so Restore purchases works.

Only if you say yes when the game asks (you can change it any time in Settings › Game):

  • How you play. Things like how far you've got, which pages you open and how often you play. It shows us where the game is confusing or too slow.
  • Crash reports. What went wrong, the game version, and whether it's iOS or Android. Crash reports don't include your account number.

Our server uses your IP address only for a moment, to stop too many requests at once. We don't store it.

What we don't collect

Your name, email, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos, health data or browsing history. There are no ads in Moonbait and no tracking across other apps. We don't sell your data. If you email us, we keep your email only to answer you.

Who we share it with

  • Apple and Google take payments. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat checks purchases for us. It gets what you bought and, if you have one, your account number.
  • DigitalOcean rents us the server where saves are kept.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and saves: your last 20 backups, until you delete your data.
  • How you play: 13 months.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase records: Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep these under their own rules.

Our server also keeps nightly backup copies for 30 days, so deleted data is fully gone from those within 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings › Account › Delete my data. This removes your account, your saves, how you played and the link to your Apple sign-in from our server right away.

No longer have the game? Email contact@notapplicablegames.com. Tell us roughly when you last played, on which kind of phone, and whether you used Sign in with Apple. Guest saves have no name on them, so we may not be able to find yours. We'll delete everything we can match to you within 30 days and email you back.

Things you bought stay with your Apple or Google account, so Restore purchases still works on a new save.

Children

Moonbait isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think your child sent us data, email us and we'll delete it.
