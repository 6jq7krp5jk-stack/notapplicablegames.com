Privacy Policy: Technically Correct

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Technically Correct ("the game") is made by Not Applicable Games (Not Applicable LLC, "we"). This policy covers the game on Steam (Windows, macOS), iOS and Android.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, nothing sold.
  • On iOS and Android the game sends anonymous play data (which courses you reach, where GARY dies, what you type at him) so we can see where players get stuck and fix those courses. It carries no name, email, account or advertising ID. It is on by default and you can turn it off at any time: SETTINGS > SHARE PLAY DATA. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks first and sends nothing unless you say yes.
  • The Steam version sends nothing.
  • The one optional purchase (unlocking the full game) is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

What the game stores on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps a small amount of information locally, using the standard preferences storage of your system (Unity "PlayerPrefs"):

  • your progress: which courses you have reached, the lessons in GARY's brain rack, the endings you have found;
  • your settings, including your SHARE PLAY DATA choice;
  • gameplay statistics for the current and previous session (courses played, lessons you typed, switches flipped, how often GARY took a surprise nap);
  • a random install ID the game makes itself (a string of letters and numbers; it is not your device's advertising ID or any other identifier from your device).

You can erase your progress at any time with RESET ALL PROGRESS on the settings page (your settings are kept), or everything by uninstalling the game.

Play data (iOS and Android)

When SHARE PLAY DATA is on, the game sends these to our server (over an encrypted HTTPS connection, roughly once a minute and when you leave the app):

  • game events: a course started, won or abandoned, GARY's deaths and their causes, the lessons you type and what the game understood them as, switches flipped, time spent, the purchase screen being shown and whether a purchase went through (never any payment details);
  • the random install ID and a random session ID, so one player's progress can be followed through the courses;
  • the game version, the platform, the device model and operating-system version, the screen size and the system language, so layout problems on particular devices can be found.

It does not include your name, email, contacts, location, photos, IP-based profile, your device's advertising identifier, or anything from outside the game. Please don't type personal information as a lesson: lessons are sent as typed.

We use play data only to improve the game (find confusing or broken courses, balance, and layout problems). It is kept on a server we run (DigitalOcean, United States), is not shared with or sold to anyone, and is deleted after 13 months. To have the data from your install deleted, email us; turning SHARE PLAY DATA off stops anything more being sent.

Tester notes (test builds only)

Test builds (TestFlight, Google Play internal testing) let testers hold two fingers on the screen to file a note. A note sends the tester's text, a screenshot of the game and the game's current state to our server. Released store builds don't have this feature.

Purchases (iOS and Android only)

On iOS and Android the game is free to try: the first wing (eight courses) is free, and a single one-time purchase unlocks the other seven wings. There are no subscriptions and nothing else to buy. The Steam version is paid up front and has no purchases inside it.

  • The purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play and processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. The game never sees your card, account, name or email.
  • To know whether you own the unlock, the game asks the App Store or Google Play, on your device, which purchases your account has made (also when you tap RESTORE PURCHASES).
  • It then keeps a small note on your device that the unlock was bought, so the game keeps working offline. That note stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall.
  • Nothing about your purchase is sent to us or to anyone else by the game.

What the game does not do

  • No personal information (name, email, contacts, location, photos, your device's own identifiers) is collected.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No subscriptions, loot boxes or consumable purchases: the only in-app purchase is the one-time full-game unlock on iOS and Android.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • The Steam version sends nothing at all.

Stores and platforms

The store you downloaded the game from (Steam, the App Store, Google Play) and your operating system may collect information under their own privacy policies, for example purchase records, crash reports you agree to send to Apple or Google, or Steam playtime. We don't control that, and we don't receive personal data from it beyond the aggregate, anonymous sales and install statistics the stores show to every developer.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's engine statistics are switched off in this game.

Children

The game is suitable for children. The anonymous play data described above contains nothing that identifies a player, child or adult; it can be switched off in SETTINGS.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
