FORM P-01 · PRIVACY DISCLOSURE

Technically Correct privacy

What leaves your device, and why. GARY has not been told.

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, nothing sold.
  • On iPhone, iPad and Android the game sends anonymous play data: which courses you reach, where GARY dies, and what you type at him. It shows us which courses are confusing so we can fix them.
  • It's on by default, and you can switch it off any time: SETTINGS › SHARE PLAY DATA. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks first and sends nothing unless you say yes.
  • The Steam version sends nothing at all.
  • The one purchase (unlocking the full game) is handled entirely by Apple or Google.
APPROVED

What stays on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps a small amount of information on your device:

  • Your progress: which courses you've reached, the lessons in GARY's brain rack, the endings you've found.
  • Your settings, including your SHARE PLAY DATA choice.
  • Play statistics for this session and the last one (courses played, lessons typed, switches flipped, naps taken).
  • An install number. A random string the game makes itself. It isn't your device's advertising ID or any other ID from your device.

Erase your progress with RESET ALL PROGRESS on the settings page (your settings are kept), or everything by uninstalling the game.

Play data (iPhone, iPad, Android)

While SHARE PLAY DATA is on, the game sends these to our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, about once a minute and when you leave the app:

  • Game events: a course started, won or abandoned; GARY's deaths and what caused them; the lessons you type and what the game understood them as; switches flipped; time spent; whether the purchase screen was shown and whether a purchase went through (never any payment details).
  • The install number and a session number, so one player's progress can be followed through the courses.
  • About the device: the game version, platform, device model, operating-system version, screen size and system language, so layout problems on particular devices can be found.

Lessons are sent exactly as typed, so please don't type personal information at GARY. He wouldn't know what to do with it anyway.

What we don't collect

Your name, email, contacts, location, photos, your device's advertising ID, or anything from outside the game. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

How it's used and kept

We use play data only to improve the game: to find confusing or broken courses, tune the difficulty, and fix layout problems. It's kept on a server we run (DigitalOcean, United States), isn't shared with or sold to anyone, and is deleted after 13 months.

To have the data from your install deleted, email contact@notapplicablegames.com. Switching SHARE PLAY DATA off stops anything more being sent.

Tester notes (test builds only)

Test builds (TestFlight and Google Play internal testing) let testers hold two fingers on the screen to file a note. A note sends the tester's text, a screenshot of the game and the game's current state to our server. Released store builds don't have this feature.

Purchases

On iPhone, iPad and Android the game is free to try: the first wing (eight courses) is free, and one one-time purchase unlocks the other seven wings. No subscriptions, nothing else to buy. The Steam version is paid up front.

  • The purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. The game never sees your card, account, name or email.
  • To know whether you own the unlock, the game asks the store, on your device, which purchases your account has made (also when you tap RESTORE PRIOR REQUISITION).
  • It keeps a small note on your device that the unlock was bought, so the game works offline.

Stores, the engine, and children

The store you got the game from and your operating system may collect information under their own privacy policies (for example purchase records or crash reports you agree to send to Apple or Google). We don't control that. The game is built with the Unity engine; Unity's engine statistics are switched off.

The game is suitable for children. The play data above contains nothing that identifies a player, child or adult, and it can be switched off in SETTINGS.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version here. Questions, or to have play data from your install deleted: contact@notapplicablegames.com.
