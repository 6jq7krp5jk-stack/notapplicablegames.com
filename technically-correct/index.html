FORM S-03 · SUPERVISOR SUPPORT DESK

Technically Correct

Teach GARY. He does exactly what you say.

Need a hand?

Email contact@notapplicablegames.com. A human reads it. GARY is not allowed near the inbox.

If something broke, tell us which course you were on (the sign at the top of the screen, like B-3) and what GARY did instead of what you wanted.

RECEIVED

I bought the full game. Where did it go?

Open the unlock screen (it appears when you head into wing B) and tap RESTORE PRIOR REQUISITION. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought it with. On Android it usually restores on its own at launch.

GARY won't do what I typed.

That's the game, mostly. He takes every word literally and files it as a lesson card. Switch cards off in his brain rack, eject the ones that are making things worse, and stop the lab clock whenever you need to think. Every course can be done; a few have some very strange solutions.

What does the game send?

On phones and tablets, anonymous play data: which courses you reach, where GARY dies, and what you type at him, so we can fix the courses people get stuck on. No name, no account, no ads. Turn it off any time in SETTINGS › SHARE PLAY DATA. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I start over?

SETTINGS › RESET ALL PROGRESS wipes your courses and GARY's brain but keeps your settings and your purchase. GARY will not remember you. He rarely does.
