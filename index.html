◆ One lane. Two armies. No second chances.

Procession Engine is a single-player roguelike about winning a war of momentum. Your cards slide along a moving belt; play them before they fall off the end. Spend them on units, buildings, and spells, and the army you assemble marches itself down the lane toward the enemy stronghold while theirs marches back at you.

Every fight is a tipping point. Hold the line, find the moment the push turns, and break through before the enemy finds theirs.

Between fights, chart a route through each act: draft new cards, collect relics, upgrade your blueprints, and shape a run that snowballs from a handful of units into a procession the enemy cannot stop.

◆ Three races, three ways to win

Bloomwardens

Living groves that seed the lane with sprouts and grow small beginnings into overwhelming numbers. Patience that turns into an avalanche.

Iron Concord

Machines built to advance. Engines, artillery, and armor that trade flesh for iron and grind the lane forward one piston-stroke at a time.

Hollow Choir

A congregation of the departed that hoards souls and spends them. Every death feeds the choir, and the choir remembers.

◆ What's in the box

  • The belt. Cards ride a conveyor toward the void. Play them, bank them, or watch them go.
  • 239 cards across units, buildings, spells, and passives, with race-defining archetypes to build around.
  • Over 300 relics that bend the rules of a run.
  • Ten Trials of ascending difficulty once you have won your first war.
  • Real armies, not stat bars. Every unit walks the lane, swings, and dies in full view.
  • A route to chart each act: fights, shops, shrines, and choices that commit before you see the reward.
