The game

A war of momentum, fought in real time

Procession Engine is a single-player roguelike deckbuilder where nothing waits for you. Cards ride a moving belt toward the edge. Your buildings send an army marching down two lanes while the enemy's marches back.

There are no turns to hide in. Hold your line, find the moment the push turns, and break their stronghold before they break yours. Between fights, carve a route through three acts, draft cards and relics, upgrade your blueprints, and grow a handful of units into a procession nothing can stop.

How it plays

A deck on a timer, an army on the march

01

Your deck is a conveyor belt

Cards don't wait in a hand. They arrive on a moving belt and roll toward the edge: play them now, bank one for the right moment, or watch them expire. Passives pay out for every second they ride. It's a deckbuilder where the deck itself is on a timer.

02

Every fight is a tug of war

Your stronghold on one side, theirs on the other, two lanes between. Every unit walks, fights and falls on the field, and the enemy keeps building while you do. The fight ends when a stronghold falls, and not a moment before.

03

Build a yard, not just a deck

Your power comes from buildings as much as cards: spawners, artillery, soul engines and more, each with blueprint variants to discover and upgrade. Socket elemental imbues onto them, then etch your best at the Workshop so they are already standing when the next battle begins.

04

A campaign that fights back

Carve a path through three acts of skirmishes, elites, bazaars, strange events, relic shrines and workshops. Each act belongs to a different enemy faction and ends at one of its two bosses, and your stronghold's scars carry from one fight to the next.

Invest in the Lattice

Every node you clear ticks a web of run-long investments: gold, provisions for every fight to come, or a sharper eye for rare cards. Small choices early compound by act three.

Make the run yours

Card rewards, relics, blueprints, tempering and etched buildings all bend the same run. With hundreds of cards and relics, no two processions march alike.

Climb the Trials

Win, and the rules turn against you. Ten stacking Trials twist the war further each time, until winning at all is the achievement.
