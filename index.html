The game

A war of momentum, fought in real time

Procession Engine is a roguelike deckbuilder where nothing waits for you. Cards ride a moving belt toward the edge. Your buildings send an army marching down two lanes while the enemy's marches back.

There are no turns to hide in. Hold your line, find the moment the push turns, and break their stronghold before they break yours. Between fights, carve a route through three acts, draft cards and relics, upgrade your blueprints, and grow a handful of units into a procession nothing can stop.

How it plays

A deck on a timer, an army on the march

01

Your deck is a conveyor belt

You don't play one hero. Your buildings spawn your army and your cards decide what it becomes: raise a new spawner mid-fight, arm a whole lane, or break a line with a spell. Cards ride a moving belt toward the edge, so play them or lose them.

02

Every fight is a tug of war

Your stronghold on one side, theirs on the other, two lanes between. Armies march both ways, meet in the middle, and try to push through to the opposing stronghold. There are no turns. The enemy keeps building while you do, and the fight ends when a stronghold falls.

03

Build a yard, not just a deck

54 buildings, from spawners and artillery to soul engines, each with three blueprint variants to find and upgrade. Socket elemental imbues onto them. Etch your best at the Workshop and they are already standing when the next fight begins.

04

A campaign that fights back

Carve a path through three acts of skirmishes, elites, bazaars, strange events, relic shrines and workshops. Each act belongs to a different enemy faction and ends at one of its two bosses.

Invest in the Lattice

Over 250 cards and over 250 relics, blueprints, tempering and the Lattice's run-long investments mean no two processions march alike.

Make the run yours

Card rewards, relics, blueprints, tempering and etched buildings all bend the same run.

Climb the Trials

Win, and the rules turn against you. Ten stacking Trials, each twisting the war further, until winning at all is the achievement.
